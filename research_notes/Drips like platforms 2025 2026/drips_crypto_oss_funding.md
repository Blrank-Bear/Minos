# Drips Network and crypto-native OSS funding / contributor-reward platforms (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-10-28. About 28 tool calls. Sources are labelled as primary (the project's own pages), forum (governance posts), or secondary (media, aggregators, AI summaries, third-party guides). "Verified" means a primary or independent source reports something that already happened. "Claimed" means marketing copy, a plan or a proposal.

## Q1: What Drips offers in 2025–2026, how individual contributors earn, typical payouts

### Takeaway
Drips (Public Goods Association / Radworks) started as streaming, splits and Drip Lists. In 2025 it added RetroPGF round tooling (used for Filecoin's 580k+ FIL round). Its big new contributor-facing product is **Drips Wave**, launched with the Stellar Development Foundation (SDF). Wave is a monthly ~7-day sprint: contributors solve maintainer-scoped GitHub issues, earn Points, and share a $60K–$75K pool pro rata. Payouts are in USDC on Stellar, and KYC is required to withdraw. Ten Stellar Waves are listed for 2026 (January to October), with about $705K in combined budgets. Stellar is the only Wave ecosystem found so far.

### Cited Findings
**Product timeline**
- Q1 2025: Drips went live on Optimism and Metis. The Optimism launch came with a "Drips for Privacy" campaign with web3privacy. The Metis deployment distributes proceeds from Metis's Retroactive Public Goods Funding. The app also gained universal search. — [Drips Q1 2025 roundup](https://www.drips.network/blog/posts/q1-2025-roundup); [Radworks forum Q1 2025](https://community.radworks.org/t/drips-org-q1-2025-update/3638)
- Q2 2025: Drips announced end-to-end RetroPGF support (nomination, voting, payouts) and previewed "Drips AI" and an "Ecosystems" feature. — [Radworks forum Q2 2025](https://community.radworks.org/t/drips-org-q2-2025-update/3652)
- Q3 2025: RetroPGF launched on Drips and is being used for Filecoin's largest round to date (580k+ FIL). Drips also open-sourced "Gardener", released a new JS SDK and proposed a contract upgrade. — [Radworks forum Q3 2025](https://community.radworks.org/t/drips-org-q3-2025-update/3687)
- Wave announcement post (Dec 23, 2025): "Stellar Wave 1" is the first full Wave, in January 2026. Contributors who signed up before Jan 11, 2026 got 100 bonus points. Contributors sync GitHub and pick scoped issues; the platform tracks work, handles review flow and issues rewards automatically. — [Drips blog: What's launching in January](https://www.drips.network/blog/posts/drips-wave-whats-launching-in-january); [Drips on X](https://x.com/dripsnetwork/status/2005985559836254654)
- Drips describes Wave as "a one-week bounty sprint that repeats each month": maintainers nominate issues, contributors pick them up, and everyone competes for one shared reward pool. — [Drips on X](https://x.com/dripsnetwork/status/2005985553557406038); [Wave solution page](https://www.drips.network/solutions/wave)
- Q1 2026 forum update (Apr 22, 2026):
  - The Stellar Wave brought 1,000+ new developers to Stellar, and 30,000+ PRs were merged across the program.
  - Wave 4 launched Apr 22, 2026 with a larger budget than earlier Waves.
  - Wave revenue "roughly cover[s] monthly personnel costs" for Drips.
  - The Drips protocol was upgraded to use Lit Protocol proofs for off-chain identity claims.
  - UNICEF joined as a funder of public goods. This is a pilot, not a Wave.
  - Filecoin's next round, RPGF-4, is planned on Drips.
  — [Radworks forum Q1 2026](https://community.radworks.org/t/drips-org-q1-2026-update/3721); [Drips Q1 2026 roundup](https://www.drips.network/blog/posts/q1-2026-roundup)

**How contributors earn**
- Rewards are split by each eligible contributor's share of Points. — [Stellar Wave page](https://www.drips.network/wave/stellar)
- Third-party guide (secondary): an issue is worth 100, 150 or 200 points depending on complexity. Payouts are USDC on Stellar, with a $1 minimum and 1–3 business days of latency. — [gigs.sh Drips Wave guide](https://gigs.sh/p/drips-wave)
- Earnings are "Reward Grants" in USDC on Stellar. Withdrawing needs approved KYC (Settings → Profile) and a Stellar wallet that supports USDC. — [Drips docs: Withdrawing rewards](https://docs.drips.network/wave/withdrawing-rewards/)
- Participation is free for maintainers. Wave Organizers such as SDF fund the pool. — [Drips docs: Participating in a Wave](https://docs.drips.network/wave/maintainers/participating-in-a-wave/)

**Terms & Rules**
- Eligibility:
  - You must be 18 or older (or the local age of majority).
  - People in, or residents of, comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions are excluded, as are people on sanctions or denied-party lists and people in FATF non-cooperative areas.
  - One account per natural person.
  - KYC/KYB may be required before rewards are allocated.
- Points:
  - Points have no monetary value.
  - Each Wave has a capped total pool.
  - The Points-to-token rate is set at the Association's discretion.
  - A per-participant cap is possible (no figure given).
  - Part of the pool may go to maintainers outside the Points calculation.
- Payment: rewards are "generally" paid in the organizer's token, though other crypto or fiat is possible. Gas fees may apply.
- AI use: low-quality, untested or not-understood LLM output is prohibited. AI use itself is not banned.
- Repeated issue-assignment requests are a prohibited behaviour.
— [Drips Wave Terms & Rules](http://docs.drips.network/wave/terms-and-rules/)

**Typical payouts (secondary only)**
- Jan–Apr 2026: $255K disbursed across 4 Waves.
- The latest Wave at the time of writing had 782 contributors.
- The top of the leaderboard earns single-digit thousands of USDC, the long tail under $100.
- The guide estimates $50–$2,500 per cycle for active contributors.
— [gigs.sh](https://gigs.sh/p/drips-wave)
- Drips' own aggregate claim: "$400k+ distributed, 50k+ issues resolved, 200+ repos". This conflicts with the Stellar page counter (372,399 issues, 842 repos) and with the $255K figure, so the numbers come from different dates or definitions. — [search summary of Drips Wave pages](https://www.drips.network/solutions/wave), contradicted by [Stellar Wave page](https://www.drips.network/wave/stellar)

**Other ecosystems**
- No Wave partner other than Stellar was found. An Optimism-branded Wave solution page exists. — [optimism.drips.network/solutions/wave](https://optimism.drips.network/solutions/wave)
- Past Drips collaborators include Filecoin, the Ethereum Foundation, ENS, UNICEF and Scroll. Radworks streamed $1M through Drips to FOSS dependencies in 2023–24. — [gigs.sh](https://gigs.sh/p/drips-wave) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Wave is the only Drips product that pays individual contributors directly per issue. Streams, splits, Drip Lists and RetroPGF mostly fund projects or maintainers.
- At a $75K pool and roughly 800 contributors, the average payout is about $95 per Wave (my estimate), with heavy concentration at the top. Expect the per-hour return for casual contributors to be low.
- The 100-point sign-up bonus and per-issue points create an incentive to farm many small issues, which is presumably why "repeated assignment requests" and low-quality LLM work are banned.

### Gaps

- Whether an 11th Stellar Wave (Nov 2026+) or a non-Stellar Wave is planned.
- The official points table: 100/150/200 comes only from a third-party guide.
- Exact countries excluded by KYC.

## Q2: Drips-like platforms launched or opened publicly in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Few genuinely new contribute-to-earn platforms launched in 2025–26. Most activity was existing ecosystems switching mechanisms: algorithmic or metric-based retro rewards (Optimism, Talent Builder Rewards), AI or jury dependency weighting (Deep Funding), monthly builder contests (Celo Proof of Ship, Stacks via Talent), plus token-based package rewards (tea mainnet, June 2026). The closest analogues to Drips Wave for individual contributors are Talent Protocol Builder Rewards (Base, WalletConnect, Stacks), Merit Systems (stablecoin payouts to repo contributors, launched July 2025), and OnlyDust (an older platform now transitioning).

### Cited Findings

**New in 2025–2026**

- **Merit Systems / Terminal** (merit.systems)
  - Raised $10M in Jan 2025, led by a16z crypto and Blockchain Capital. — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/01/16/merit-systems-raises-10m-create-new-economic-model-called-open-source-capitalism/)
  - Launched the Terminal and a payments beta on Jul 2, 2025. Repo owners surface and pay high-impact contributors with stablecoins, and Merit says it handles taxes end-to-end. — [Merit blog launch](https://www.merit.systems/blog/launch); [Privy case study](https://privy.io/blog/turning-open-source-into-businesses-with-merit)
  - Payout volume: none found.
  - Mechanism: repo owners pay contributors per contribution, scored by impact.

- **Deep Funding** (deepfunding.org; Vitalik / Gitcoin)
  - Pilot: $250K from Vitalik, split $170K to OSS projects by dependency-graph weights and two $40K prizes for AI models. — [Gitcoin mechanism page](https://gitcoin.co/mechanisms/deep-funding)
  - The pilot mapped about 40K Ethereum dependencies. Results were scheduled for Feb 27, 2025; I did not confirm publication. — [Gitcoin forum visual guide](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/deep-funding-a-visual-guide-in-3-easy-steps/19765)
  - In GG24 (Oct 2025), about $350K went via Deep Funding using Seer prediction markets. — [GG24 case study](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/gg24-first-funding-round-of-gitcoin-3-0)
  - Mechanism: funds go to projects or repos, not to individual contributors directly.

- **tea protocol** (tea.xyz)
  - Mainnet set for Jun 4, 2026, with a TEA listing on Aerodrome. — [Whale Alert story](https://whale-alert.io/stories/f5b301bbd524d6/Tea-protocol-to-launch-mainnet-June-4-and-hold-TEA-token-listing-on-Aerodrome-after-Aerodrome-vote-on-May-28); [tea.xyz](https://tea.xyz/)
  - A CMC AI summary (secondary, AI-generated) says mainnet v1 launched in June 2026 as an OP Stack L2. — [CoinMarketCap AI](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/tea-protocol/latest-updates/)
  - Mechanism: "Proof of Contribution" with a teaRank score from 0 to 100 that rewards package maintainers (npm, Homebrew) in TEA tokens. 28% of supply goes to contributor and maintainer incentives and airdrops. — [Bitget Academy](https://www.bitget.com/academy/what-is-tea-protocol-tea-token-presale-september-25-2025)
  - Red flag: the 2024 testnet was hit by a repository spam attack. — [devclass](https://devclass.com/2024/03/04/test-launch-of-tea-open-source-reward-project-clouded-by-repository-spam-attack)
  - Rewards are a speculative token, and the mainnet launch is not confirmed by a primary source here.

- **Talent Protocol Builder Rewards** (talent.app), with Base, WalletConnect and Stacks as funders. All 2025 data.
  - Base edition: 2 ETH a week split across the top ~100 builders. Eligibility needs a Builder Score of 40+, built from GitHub, onchain activity and other data. — [Base docs: get funded](https://docs.base.org/get-started/get-funded); [Talent guide](https://talentprotocol.notion.site/builder-rewards-guide?pvs=4)
  - Base pilot: about 200 builders paid 8 ETH over 4 weeks, with 72% retention (Talent's founder, self-reported). — [paragraph @macedo](https://paragraph.xyz/@macedo/my-q1-journey)
  - WalletConnect edition: 1M WCT in total, 75K WCT a week to the top 100, Sep 30–Nov 11, 2025, scored on GitHub activity plus usage. — [WalletConnect FAQ](https://docs.talent.app/docs/legal/builder-rewards-faq-walletconnect)
  - Stacks Builder Challenges:
    - Dec 10–30, 2025: Week 1 had a 5,000 STX pool. — [KuCoin flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/stacks-partners-with-talent-protocol-for-weekly-builder-challenge-focused-on-clarity-4-bitcoin-apps); [Stacks blog](https://www.stacks.co/blog/stacks-builder-challenges-stx-rewards)
    - Jan 19–31, 2026: 15,300 STX, with the top 10 sharing half the pool. The wallet is linked on talent.app and GitHub tracking is used. — [Talent docs: Stacks Builder Challenges](https://docs.talent.app/docs/legal/stacks-builder-challenges)
  - A "Summer League" with 20 ETH was announced (date unknown). Current 2026 Base status is unknown.

- **Celo Proof of Ship** (Celo DevRel with Karma GAP)
  - A monthly contest. Rewards are computed from Karma profile metrics (transactions, users, GitHub README and commits, demo video) and claimed via Builder Score. — [Karma GAP docs](https://docs.gap.karmahq.xyz/how-to-guides/partners/celo-proof-of-ship)
  - Round 7 offered 15K CELO plus 5K cUSD; Round 9 offered 20K CELO. — [Celo devs newsletter PoS 7](https://celo-devs.beehiiv.com/p/proof-of-ship-7-2nd-milestone-checkin); [PoS 9](https://celo-devs.beehiiv.com/p/proof-of-ship-9-2nd-milestone-checkin)
  - The program is marketed as "earn up to 15k monthly". — [newsletter](https://celo-devs.beehiiv.com/p/prove-your-impact-on-celo-earn-up-to-15k-monthly)
  - It rewards app builders, not contributions to other people's repos.

- **NEAR Protocol Rewards** (near-horizon)
  - An npm or GitHub tool scores GitHub activity (50%) and onchain activity (50%), with weekly tiers from $500 (Bronze) to $2,500 (Diamond). — [npm docs](https://unpkg.com/near-protocol-rewards@1.1.2/docs/rewards.md); [GitHub repo](https://github.com/near-horizon/near-protocol-rewards)
  - In Q2 2025 it evolved into a cohort program and paid $200K+ to 30+ teams. — [Nansen Q2 2025](https://research.nansen.ai/articles/near-quarterly-report-q2-2025)
  - It rewards teams, and an official current status page was not found.

- **Stellar Build Challenge (7th edition)**
  - Rewards new contributions to meaningful Stellar OSS projects. This is separate from SCF. — [Stellar blog](https://stellar.org/blog/announcing-the-7th-stellar-build-challenge)

- **Token-paid PR programs** (low quality, secondary only)
  - Aigen Protocol pays 50 AIGEN per PR. MergeOS pays 300 MRG per PR verification, and its token later dropped.
  - All figures are anecdotal and self-reported, and both are red flags for token speculation. — [dev.to Open Source Money Map](https://dev.to/zeroknowledge0x/the-open-source-money-map-every-way-developers-are-actually-making-money-in-2026-with-real-45ba)
  - Opire (bounties on any GitHub issue, paid via crypto or Stripe) is mentioned in the same body of 2026 blogs. Its launch date was not verified. — [dev.to bounties guide](https://dev.to/timmothybuilder/how-to-find-and-win-open-source-bounties-in-2026-2b4b)

**Older platforms: what changed in 2025–26**

- **OnlyDust** (Fabric Ventures-backed; 2022–)
  - Its rewards dashboard says it is "transitioning to our new platform for all open source projects" and asks users to withdraw pending rewards. — [app.onlydust.com rewards](https://app.onlydust.com/my-dashboard/rewards)
  - OnlyDust Fellowship: $300–$5,000 a month for 3 months, selected manually. — [OnlyDust docs](https://docs.onlydust.com/contributors-hiya/onlydust-fellowship)
  - Historical scale: $11M+ paid to 10K developers in its first 2 years (self-reported). — [search summary](https://github.com/gitcoinco/gitcoin_co_30/issues/396)
  - The name of the new platform was not found.

- **Gitcoin**
  - Grants Stack was retired at the end of May 2025, and "Gitcoin 3.0" became a multi-mechanism "domains" model. — [Gitcoin 3.0 strategy sprint](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/gitcoin-3-0-gitcoin-grants-3-0-strategy-sprint/20384)
  - GG24 (Oct 14–28, 2025) distributed about $1.8M across 6 domains: about $1.175M from Gitcoin and $632.5K from partners. — [GG24 case study](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/gg24-first-funding-round-of-gitcoin-3-0)
  - The Dev Tooling domain (~$600K) used Deep Funding (~$350K) and QF on Giveth (~$200K, 55 projects).
  - OSS QF on Giveth: $300K in matching for 64 projects from 1,286 donors. — [Giveth retrospective](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/gg24-oss-qf-giveth-retrospective)
  - A 2026 draft proposal would sunset Grants in its current form for a year-long single-domain "d/acc" campaign, targeting a May 2026 start. It is unconfirmed. — [Gitcoin forum proposal](https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/proposal-gitcoin-d-acc-2026-funding-initiative-restructuring-the-grants-program/25157)
  - Recipients are projects, not individuals.

- **Optimism Retro Funding**
  - In 2025, rounds became continuous, metric- and algorithm-based monthly "Missions".
  - Dev Tooling: up to 3.65M OP in S8. 6.6M OP had been awarded to dev tools by Jul 2025 across 5 of 6 monthly periods.
  - Onchain Builders: up to 1.35M OP in S8.
  - A new OP Stack dependencies mission was proposed.
  — [S8 Retro Funding Missions](https://gov.optimism.io/t/s8-retro-funding-missions/10003); [S7 early evidence](https://gov.optimism.io/t/season-7-retro-funding-early-evidence-on-developer-tooling-impact/10162)
  - Rewards go to projects (repos qualify via OSO metrics), not individuals. 2026 rules were not found.

- **Octant v2** (Golem Foundation)
  - A yield-based "Sustainability Pool" fed by "Dragon" vaults. Golem stakes 100K ETH. — [Octant v2 summary](https://octantapp.notion.site/octantv2-ecosystem-growth-engine); [Octant intro](https://gov.optimism.io/t/introducing-octant/9361)
  - No firm 2025 launch date was found.
  - Funds projects.

- **Stellar Community Fund**
  - v6.0 merged its awards into a Build Award (up to $150K in XLM, in tranches) plus a Kickstart Award (up to $15K).
  - v7.0 adds three build-award tracks with rolling invites. AI screening and referral incentives are reported by a single secondary source.
  - The SCF #41 deadline conflicts: Feb 1 vs Feb 8, 2026.
  — [Introducing SCF v7](https://stellar.org/blog/ecosystem/introducing-scf-v7); [SCF v6](https://stellar.org/blog/developers/introducing-stellar-community-fund-v6-0); [SCF awards](https://communityfund.stellar.org/awards); [blockchain.news](https://blockchain.news/zh/news/stellar-scf-7-grant-system-ai-screening-referrals)
  - It funds teams; Drips Wave is SDF's individual-contributor channel.

- **Algora** (bounties in USD or USDC, $50–$500, ~15% merge rate per one blogger) — [dev.to](https://dev.to/zeroknowledge0x/the-open-source-money-map-every-way-developers-are-actually-making-money-in-2026-with-real-45ba)
  - Pre-2025 and not crypto-native.

### Inferences
**Ranked by fit for "individual developer earns per OSS contribution"**
1. Drips Wave (Stellar): an open pool, per issue, USDC, KYC required. The best documented.
2. Talent Builder Rewards and Stacks challenges: weekly top-N leaderboards, needing Builder Score and GitHub. Payouts are in crypto tokens (ETH, WCT, STX).
3. Merit Systems: depends on a repo owner choosing to pay.
4. OnlyDust: transitioning.
5. tea: token-based, aimed at package maintainers.

Optimism Retro, Gitcoin GG24, Deep Funding, Octant, SCF, Celo PoS and NEAR fund projects or teams, so an individual contributor benefits only by maintaining or owning a funded project.

**Red flags**
- Points that convert at a discretionary rate (Drips Terms allow this).
- Native-token payouts (tea, AIGEN, MRG, STX, WCT) with price risk.
- Leaderboard winner-take-most splits (Talent: top 10 get 50%).
- Self-reported metrics everywhere.

### Gaps
- Protocol Guild 2025–26 changes, Hypercerts, Allo successors, Ethereum Foundation contributor programs, Farcaster/Neynar dev rewards, Giveth standalone QF rounds, and Base Builder Rewards status in 2026: not researched, because of the tool-call budget.
- OnlyDust's new platform name and status.
- Whether the tea mainnet actually launched and is paying, and its spam controls in 2026.
- Official Merit payout volume.
- Optimism Retro Funding rules for 2026.
- Whether the Gitcoin d/acc 2026 restructuring was adopted.
