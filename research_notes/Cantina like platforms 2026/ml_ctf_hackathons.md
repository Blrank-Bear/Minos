# ML / Data Competition, Prediction-Tournament, CTF and Hackathon Platforms: New in 2026 and Major 2026 Changes

Research date: 2026-10-28. About 17 search/fetch calls. Overall: **I found no clearly new standalone ML-competition, CTF or web3-hackathon *platform* that launched in 2026 and has verified payouts.** Most of the 2026 activity is new *programs* or *format changes* at platforms that already existed: Kaggle Community Hackathons, ARC Prize 2026, the CodaLab-to-Codabench move, Numerai Risk, and the Gray Swan Arena relaunch.

## Q1: New ML/data competition or prediction-tournament platforms in 2026 (including leads to verify: Codabench, CompeteHub, CompeteX)

### Takeaway
The mlcontests.com 2025 report covers 390+ competitions and over $16M in prizes across 30+ platforms. It lists no platform launched in 2025 or 2026; the newest in its table is Codabench (2023). Codabench is confirmed as CodaLab's successor. The "CompeteHub" lead is a general student-competition discovery site, not an ML platform. "CompeteX" returned no results at all.

## Q2: Notable 2026 changes at established platforms (Kaggle, Zindi, DrivenData, AIcrowd, Numerai, CrunchDAO, Topcoder, SIGNATE)

### Takeaway
- **Kaggle:** launched Community Hackathons (self-serve, prizes up to $10k, free to the host) in March 2026. It also hosts ARC Prize 2026 ($2M total across three tracks).
- **Numerai:** raised $30M at a $500M valuation, changed payout rules from 1 Jan 2026, announced a $1M NMR buyback and launched a new "Numerai Risk" tournament.
- **No 2026 news found** for Zindi, DrivenData, AIcrowd, Topcoder or SIGNATE.

### Cited Findings
- **Kaggle Community Hackathons** (article dated 19 Mar 2026):
  - Self-serve: hosts define the challenge, upload data, set tracks, timelines and judges.
  - Submissions can include apps, videos and reports, not just leaderboard scores.
  - Anyone can host, from student clubs to nonprofits, startups and corporate teams.
  - Prizes go up to $10k per hackathon, offered "at no cost to the host."

  Source: [Gadgetbond](https://gadgetbond.com/kaggle-community-hackathons-launch/). This is secondary press; I did not find Kaggle's own announcement.
- **Kaggle changes in 2025, per mlcontests:**
  - Community Competitions can now offer prizes up to $10k; 34 of Kaggle's 68 competitions in 2025 were this type.
  - New features: Benchmarks, Game Arena and Writeups.
  - The progression system (Master and Grandmaster criteria) was overhauled.

  Source: [mlcontests](https://mlcontests.com/state-of-machine-learning-competitions-2025/)
- **ARC Prize 2026** runs on Kaggle with $2M total across three tracks. Code must be open-sourced under a permissive licence (CC0 or MIT-0), and there is no internet access during evaluation. Kaggle submission deadline is 2 Nov 2026; results on 4 Dec 2026. — [arcprize.org 2026](https://arcprize.org/competitions/2026)
  - **ARC-AGI-2 track: $700k.** Breakdown: $275k progress prizes, $275k grand-prize write-up awards, and a $150k bonus for the first solution scoring 85% or higher (rolls over to 2027 if no one reaches it). 2026 is the final year ARC-AGI-2 runs as an official Kaggle competition. — [ARC-AGI-2 page](https://arcprize.org/competitions/2026/arc-agi-2)
    - **Conflict:** a staging subdomain shows a different split: a $500k guaranteed top-score award plus a $200k bonus for 85% or higher. — [newhotness.arcprize.org](https://newhotness.arcprize.org/competitions/2026/arc-agi-2)
  - **ARC-AGI-3 track: $850k.** A $700k grand prize for the first agent to score 100%, plus a $75k top-score award and a $75k milestone pool. — [ARC-AGI-3 page](https://arcprize.org/competitions/2026/arc-agi-3)
  - **Paper Prize: $450k.** A $75k guaranteed top-paper award plus a $375k pool for outstanding papers. Papers must be tied to a Kaggle code submission. — [Paper Prize](https://arcprize.org/competitions/2026/paper)
- **Other 2026 Kaggle competitions:**
  - Enveda CASMI 2026: entry deadline 7 Dec 2026. — [Digg Kaggle feed](https://digg.com/u/kaggle) (aggregator)
  - NeuroGolf 2026: final submissions due 15 Jul 2026. — [IJCAI 2026](https://2026.ijcai.org/?p=2694)
- **Numerai:**
  - **Funding:** $30M Series C at a $500M valuation, led by university endowments.
  - **Payout rules:** a new payout target took effect by 1 Jan 2026, and the Signals payout clip rose from 1.7% to 3.5%.
  - **Monthly payouts:** about $251,850 in NMR for November 2025.

  Source: [Numerai Dec 2025 update](https://blog.numer.ai/numerai-december-2025-update/)
- **Numerai, later in 2026:**
  - Paid about $182,039 for January 2026.
  - Announced a $1M NMR buyback and a new tournament, **Numerai Risk**, in which models forecast residual volatility.

  Source: [Numerai blog (NumerCon / Risk / buyback post)](https://blog.numer.ai/numerai-monthly-numercon-agents-staking-risk-1m-nmr-buyback.md). This is the company's own blog, but payouts are tracked on-chain and in the community.
- **Zindi:** added multi-metric evaluation and new competition types in 2025; 28 competitions with $226k in prizes. — [mlcontests](https://mlcontests.com/state-of-machine-learning-competitions-2025/)
- **EvalAI:** moving to a paid model for hosting challenges and ending support for code-upload challenges. — [mlcontests](https://mlcontests.com/state-of-machine-learning-competitions-2025/)
- **Hugging Face Competitions:** last commit October 2024 and the product lead left in early 2025, so it is effectively dormant. It still hosted 2 competitions with $16k in prizes in 2025. — [mlcontests](https://mlcontests.com/state-of-machine-learning-competitions-2025/)

### Inferences
- Kaggle's March 2026 Community Hackathons extend its 2025 community-prize model (prizes up to $10k) to hackathon-style judged contests. Expect many more small-prize events with lower prestige.
- Numerai's business is growing (new funding, buyback, new tournament), but monthly NMR payouts in the data points I found fell from about $252k (November 2025) to about $182k (January 2026). That may reflect the token price or the payout-target change; I did not verify which.

### Gaps
- I found no 2026-specific announcements for Zindi, DrivenData, AIcrowd, Topcoder or SIGNATE.
- I did not find an official Kaggle blog post confirming the Community Hackathons details.
- Numerai payout figures after spring 2026 were not found.
