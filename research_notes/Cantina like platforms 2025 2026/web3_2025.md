# Cantina-like Web3 security earning platforms launched in 2025

Scope: audit-contest, bug-bounty, crowdsourced-review and AI-vs-human audit venues that were unveiled, launched, left beta or opened publicly in calendar 2025. Research date: 2026-10-28.

Method note: about 30 web searches and fetches. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so a few leads could not be checked (listed under Gaps). The search engine returns summarised snippets. Where a claim rests only on a snippet and the primary page was not opened, it is marked "(snippet)".

## Q1. Which new audit-contest, bug-bounty or security-review marketplace platforms launched (or opened publicly) in 2025?

### Takeaway
I could not confirm a single brand-new, independent, human-researcher contest or bounty platform launched in 2025 with real payouts. Most of 2025's "launches" were one of three kinds: new tiers or programs on incumbent platforms (Immunefi All Stars, Immunefi Magnus, CodeHawks Eagles and AI First Flights, Code4rena's zero-fee model); AI auditor products (Sherlock AI, Zellic V12, Veridise AuditHub, Nethermind AuditAgent); or one AI-agent bounty arena, **Nethermind AgentArena** (early access, July 2025). Remedy (Hexens) became active in 2025, but it first went live in beta in 2024.

### Cited Findings

**A. New venue with a different format: AI-agent audit arena**
- **Nethermind AgentArena** (agentarena.nethermind.io). Nethermind's EthCC[8] post (17 Jul 2025) says: "The early-access platform is now available at agentarena.nethermind.io". It "provides the space for smart contract auditing agents to compete for bounties", and protocols set those bounties. **Launch: July 2025, early access.** — [Nethermind blog, 17 Jul 2025](https://www.nethermind.io/blog/ethcc-8-2025-netherminds-contributions-to-ethereums-next-phase)
  - The docs call it "the first on-chain auditing arena designed specifically for automated auditing agents". Builders register agents, which "earn rewards based on their performance". The docs give no reward split or payout statistics. — [AgentArena docs](https://docs.agentarena.nethermind.io/docs/intro)
  - It has a Public Bounty Mode (any qualified agent can join). A TEE-based arbiter scores findings, and human auditors validate them (snippet). — [AgentArena](https://agentarena.nethermind.io/)
  - Evidence of real use: Lido ran three AI auditing bounty competitions on it. An AI arbiter scored submissions, then human auditors filtered them. Lido's "Evergreen" pilot ran four more multi-agent competitions on Lido Core. Bounty amounts were not published. — [Nethermind Lido case study](https://www.nethermind.io/blog/agent-arena-case-study-adversarial-ai-security-analysis-for-lido); [Lido Core pilot](https://www.nethermind.io/blog/piloting-continuous-ai-augmented-security-on-lido-core)
  - An ETHGlobal Buenos Aires prize track offered a $3,000 grand prize for building arena agents (snippet). — [ETHGlobal](https://ethglobal.com/events/buenosaires/prizes/nethermind)
  - Late-2026 status: the docs were updated 14 Oct 2026, so the platform is still maintained. One snippet said the landing page showed "Coming Soon", so whether it is fully public is unclear. Who can earn: AI-agent builders, not manual auditors.

**B. Platforms that became active in 2025 but launched earlier**
- **Remedy (r.xyz, by Hexens)**: bug bounty plus CTF platform. It uses ZK-proof duplicate protection and Hexens triage, and charges projects nothing.
  - It already existed in beta in **May 2024**: Glider posted that Base projects could launch bounties with "free triaging during beta (powered by @hexens)". — [Glider on X, 2024](https://x.com/glider_xyz/status/1796240448073175430)
  - **Feb 2025** push to win customers: "zero fees for projects", "Most bug bounty platforms take a cut of your payouts. Remedy doesn't." — [Remedy on X, Feb 2025](https://x.com/xyz_remedy/status/1886846545070543094); [Glider on X](https://x.com/glider_xyz/status/1886846432570957992)
  - Programs: Sora ($30k, Feb 2025) — [Remedy on X](https://x.com/xyz_remedy/status/1887811701623407007). Deq (up to $50k critical, plus up to $20k from Hexens; docs dated Apr 2025) — [Deq docs](https://docs.deq.fi/bug-bounty)
  - **Remedy CTF 2025** (24–26 Jan 2025) was Hexens' first Web3 CTF. Prizes were $21,337, $13,337 and $7,337. Remedy's recap reports $52k+ awarded and 2,845 participants; a LinkedIn post says 2,200+ participants, so the counts conflict (snippet). — [CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/event/2618); [Web3SecNews](https://web3secnews.substack.com/p/remedy-ctf-2025-heats-up-whos-leading)
  - Hexens' partner page describes Remedy as invite-only (snippet). — [Circle partner page](https://partners.circle.com/partner/hexens)
  - I found no published total payout figure.
- **HackenProof DualDefense**: an audit plus a 30-day contest and coverage pool. Hacken's CEO letter says it launched in **2024**. Many new contests ran from late 2025, for example Soda Labs ($56k, 3 Nov 2025). — [HackenProof DualDefense](https://hackenproof.com/blog/dualdefense); [Hacken CEO 2024 letter](https://wp.hacken.io/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2024-–-A-Message-from-Hackens-CEO-Dyma-Budorin.pdf)
  - Red flag from an earlier pass: a third-party snapshot found that 67 of 78 settled DualDefense audits paid $0 — [Munim snapshot](https://github.com/munimtechnologies/audit-contest-ai-rules)
- **Hashlock bug bounty platform**: an audit firm's own bounty board ("teams can list their bounty programs and tap into Hashlock's community of white hats"). I found no launch date. HackenProof also has a partnership with Hashlock. — [Hashlock bug bounty](https://hashlock.com/bug-bounty); [HackenProof x Hashlock](https://hackenproof.com/blog/hackenproof-hashlock-blockchain-security)

**C. 2025 launches inside incumbent platforms (new earning routes, not new platforms)**
- **Immunefi Magnus**, Feb 2025: an "AI-powered command center" (security operations), not a researcher venue. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/343177/immunefi-rolls-out-magnus-an-ai-powered-command-center-to-streamline-onchain-security)
- **Immunefi All Stars**, launched 19 May 2025. The Block calls it "invite-only-ish". It is an elite tier with invite-only audit work, private code and PR reviews, retainers and bounty competitions. Applications are open to qualified researchers, and bounties stay open to all. First member: LonelySloth ($3.6M earned from 60 reports). It started with 10 members, with 10 more planned. — [The Block, 19 May 2025](https://www.theblock.co/post/354815/immunefi-launches-all-stars-elite-whitehat-program-amid-record-bybit-hack-rising-onchain-losses)
- **Immunefi Attackathons in 2025**: Movement Labs (7 Mar–4 Apr 2025, up to $500k pool) — [Immunefi blog](https://immunefi.com/blog/customers/immunefi-and-movement-labs-launch-500-000-attackathon-to-secure-the-modular-move-ecosystem/). XRPL Lending Protocol with RippleX ($200k, from 13 Oct 2025, with an "Attackathon Academy" phase) (snippet) — [MEXC news](https://www.mexc.com/news/128791)
- **Code4rena zero platform fee**, 16 Jun 2025. Zellic (owner since Aug 2024) said all contests would run with no platform fee "for the foreseeable future". Prize pools and judge fees are unchanged. Zellic's stated reason is that contest platforms should be a "public good", with revenue coming from Zellic and Zenith private audits. — [Security Boulevard / Zellic, Jun 2025](https://securityboulevard.com/2025/06/code4rena-will-run-audit-contests-for-free/)
- **CodeHawks (Cyfrin) Eagles**, Mar 2025: an elite researcher tier for the highest-stakes contests and private audits (snippet of Cyfrin's 2025 wrap-up; the page returned 404 when I fetched it). — [Cyfrin 2025 wrap-up](https://www.cyfrin.io/blog/cyfrin-2025-wrap-up-advancing-web3-security-audits-and-blockchain-education)
- **CodeHawks AI First Flights**, Dec 2025 (snippet): on-demand practice audits. You can start any time, you get one week, an AI judge gives instant feedback, and there is no appeal period. This is for training, and I found no evidence of cash prizes. — [Cyfrin docs](https://docs.cyfrin.io/codehawks/ai-first-flights)
  - A third-party snapshot says no competitive CodeHawks audit started between Apr 2025 and Jul 2026 (snippet, unverified). — [Munim snapshot](https://github.com/munimtechnologies/audit-contest-ai-rules)
- **Ethereum Foundation Fusaka audit contest** ($2M, 15 Sep–13 Oct 2025), hosted on Sherlock. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/370846/ethereum-devs-open-fusaka-upgrade-to-2-million-)
- The EF bug bounty maximum rose from $250k to $1M in March 2025 (per a Sherlock article). — [Sherlock](https://sherlock.xyz/post/best-web3-bug-bounties-in-2026-the-highest-paying-programs-on-every-platform)
- The Coinbase/Base bounty moved to Cantina in July 2025 (per Sherlock). — same source

**E. Safe-harbor and on-chain bounty protocols**
- **SEAL Whitehat Safe Harbor**: a framework for rescues during live exploits, recorded on-chain through a Safe Harbor Registry. Bounties are a percentage of recovered funds with a cap (e.g., ZKsync: 10%, $1M cap, KYC required; Inverse: no KYC). I found no specific 2025 launch date; protocols adopted it through 2025 governance proposals. — [SEAL frameworks](https://frameworks.securityalliance.org/safe-harbor/overview); [ZKsync GAP-2](https://forum.zknation.io/t/gap-2-adopt-the-seal-safe-harbor-agreement/354); [Inverse docs](https://docs.inverse.finance/inverse-finance-risk/prevention/safe-harbor-agreement)
- On-chain escrow and bounty prototypes (VulnerabilityCourt and WhiteFlag on GenLayer testnet; Bug Buster on Cartesi) are GitHub projects with no production use or payouts found. — [VulnerabilityCourt](https://github.com/9ytshade/vulnerabilitycourt); [WhiteFlag](https://github.com/kenil1710/whiteflag); [Bug Buster](https://github.com/crypto-bug-hunters/bug-buster)

### Inferences
- 2025 was a consolidation year, not a launch year. Incumbents added elite tiers (Immunefi All Stars, CodeHawks Eagles) and AI tooling. Zellic stopped charging contest fees, which likely made it harder for new contest platforms to make money. That fits the absence of new independent contest platforms.
- AgentArena is the only 2025 venue I found that resembles an "AI-vs-human audit arena with bounties". Its earners are AI-agent builders, and its visible demand came mostly from one client (Lido).
- For a human researcher, the genuinely new 2025 earning routes were the elite tiers on existing platforms, plus Remedy as a smaller bounty board with Hexens triage.

### Gaps
- The search budget ran out before I could check these: crowdfunding/seed rounds for 2025 security-crowdsourcing startups (Crunchbase, RootData); regional platforms (Korea, Vietnam, China, India, Turkey) launched in 2025; firm spin-offs (Guardian, OtterSec, Zokyo, SlowMist, Beosin, Verichains, ExVul, Halborn, Quantstamp, Certora contests); Product Hunt launches; "The Web3 Security Labs" (the page fetched only a title; no launch date or payouts found); Procur3 (an audit RFP marketplace, date unknown); Ackee "Wake Arena"; Octane and Almanax (AI security startups, probably not earning venues).
- I found no official launch date for Remedy's bounty platform (evidence points to a 2024 beta) or for Hashlock's bounty board.
- AgentArena's reward splits, total bounties paid, and whether it is fully public (it may still be early access) are unverified.
- I could not open Cyfrin's 2025 wrap-up page (404), so the Eagles (Mar 2025) and AI First Flights (Dec 2025) dates rest on search snippets.
- I found no evidence of any 2025 audit-insurance and contest hybrid other than DualDefense (2024) and Sherlock's existing coverage model.
