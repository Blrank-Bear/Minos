# General (Web2 / mixed) crowdsourced-security earning platforms launched 2025–2026

Scope note: about 36 tool calls. The session's shared web-search budget ran out before every lead could be checked, so several leads below are still unverified. Launch dates were especially hard to find: most small platforms don't publish them, and several sites returned HTTP 403 or refused the connection.

## 1. New commercial bug bounty / VDP / crowdsourced pentest platforms launched 2025–2026

### Takeaway
I found **no well-documented new general-purpose commercial bug bounty platform launched in 2025 or 2026** with press coverage, funding news or evidence of payouts. Searches for "launches/unveils bug bounty platform 2025/2026", for funding rounds and on Reddit only turned up programs on existing platforms (HackerOne, Bugcrowd, Intigriti, YesWeHack). The only new-platform signals are directory additions in 2026 with no launch date (BBHunt Japan, BugStream Ukraine, OQDOPPI Uzbekistan, UzHunter, plus others) and one self-promotional pre-launch post (Hackers Guild).

### Cited Findings
**Search result (negative evidence)**
- CSO Online's "9 top bug bounty programs launched in 2025" lists only vendor programs or programs on existing platforms: Apple (revamped, effective Nov 2025, $2M top reward), the Google AI VRP (Oct 2025, $5k–$30k), Samsung mobile (up to $1M), Anthropic (HackerOne, May 2025), OpenAI (Bugcrowd, critical cap raised to $100k in Mar 2025), Nvidia (Intigriti, summer 2025). It names no new platform. — [CSO Online](https://www.csoonline.com/article/657751/top-bug-bounty-programs.html)
- The gokensai/Kensai "Complete Directory of 100+ platforms" (updated Mar 2026) has 99 unique entries. None has a founding year and none is flagged as new. — [Kensai directory](https://gokensai.com/blog/bugbountysystems/)

**disclose.io directory: 2026 additions (the best lead source)**
- The disclose/bug-bounty-platforms GitHub repo added many platforms during 2026: "Add 6 missing" (Mar 18), "Add 11 platforms" (Apr 18), "add 10" (Jun 13), "+12 verified" (Jul 5), BBHunt Japan (Jul 5, PR #67), 360 SRC and HackAPrompt (Jul 5), and **BugStream (Ukraine)** (Sep 14, 2026, PR #73). Being added to the list does NOT mean the platform launched in 2026. Many additions are old platforms (for example 360 SRC). — [disclose commits](https://github.com/disclose/bug-bounty-platforms/commits/main)
- The current disclose list has 73 "General crowdsourced" platforms. Entries not among the "already known" set that may be recent: BBHunt Japan, BugStream (UA), BugRakshak (IN), Cyber3ra (IN), Cyberbay (HK), Find The Gap (KR), Genbounty (UK), Hackr.fi (FI), HuntersPay (BR), TrustLine (SA), TumarOne (KZ), UzHunter, ZeroDay Test (BD), Bug Zero (LK), Kolahsefid (IR). The government section adds Qatar Bug Bounty (bugbounty.qa) and UAE National Bug Bounty (bugbounty.ae). — [disclose README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/disclose/bug-bounty-platforms/main/README.md)

**Per-platform checks**
- **BBHunt Japan (bbhunt.jp)** says it is "Japan's first fully localized bug bounty platform". It runs bug bounty programs and VDPs and verifies researcher identity. The site gives no legal entity, launch date, reward amounts, stats or payout evidence. It joined the disclose list in Jul 2026. Status: **claimed, unverified**. Red flags: anonymous operator, no stats. ("Japan's first" also conflicts with BugBounty.jp, which launched in Mar 2016.) — [bbhunt.jp](https://bbhunt.jp); [Security Affairs on BugBounty.jp 2016](https://securityaffairs.com/46244/hacking/sprout-bug-bounty-program.html)
- **BugStream (bugstream.com.ua, Ukraine)** is a "bug bounty and security audit platform" focused on critical infrastructure and the public sector. Named clients: National Bank of Ukraine, Prozorro, NAIS. Testimonials come from a State Enterprise and a Ministry of Defence agency. The site shows no operator entity, launch date, reward amounts or payout figures. — [bugstream.com.ua](https://bugstream.com.ua)
  - The DOU calendar lists "Bug Bash DOT-Chain 2026", organized by Ukraine's National Cybersecurity Coordination Center (NCCC/НКЦК) under the NSDC. Vulnerability testing ran on the "crowdsecurity platform BugStream" and participants were promised rewards. This points to state-linked use in 2026. — [DOU event](https://dou.ua/calendar/56405/) (seen through a search snippet; the page returned 403)
  - Context: Ukraine's government approved a legal bug bounty procedure for state bodies and private companies, drafted by the State Service of Special Communications (DOU gives the date only as "16 May"; the year was not visible). — [DOU news](https://dou.ua/lenta/news/bug-bounty-in-ukraine/)
- **OQDOPPI (oqdoppi.uz, Uzbekistan)** calls itself a "local bug bounty platform for Uzbekistan's digital security" (in search snippets, "Uzbekistan's Elite Bug Bounty Platform"). It pays in soʻm or to a bank card within 48h of a fix, scores severity with CVSS 4.0, and offers open and private programs. Its footer says © 2026. It names no operator, launch date, stats ("thousands" of hackers is a vague claim) or government backing. Status: **claimed, unverified; possibly 2025–26**. — [oqdoppi.uz](https://oqdoppi.uz/)
- **UzHunter (uzhunter.com)** is on the disclose list with no region. The site returned 403. No data. — [disclose README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/disclose/bug-bounty-platforms/main/README.md)
- **Hackers Guild** is described in a self-promotional Medium post as a "new platform" due for pre-release on 4 Jan 2025. A search found no independent trace, and its URL and operation could not be confirmed. Status: **claimed, unverified; possibly vapor**. — [Medium post](https://hackers-guild-blog.medium.com/top-8-bug-bounty-platforms-in-2025-84a1f6b7b226)
- **Genbounty (UK)** now presents itself as an "application safety platform for post-market monitoring of AI products" (EU AI Act, PLD, DORA). It offers private bug bounties for AI agents with "vetted researcher teams". The site gives no founding date, payouts or stats. — [genbounty.com](https://genbounty.com/); [Genbounty AI agents page](https://genbounty.com/bug-bounty-for-ai-agents)
- **TumarOne (tumar.one, Kazakhstan)** is NOT new. It grew out of a national bug bounty pilot idea from late 2020 with Kazakhstan's Information Security Committee and was developed by TSARKA GROUP. It is active in 2025: Bereke Bank launched a program there in Aug 2025, and Kcell raised its maximum reward to $1,000 in May 2025 after about 60 reports in the first year. — [vc.ru](https://vc.ru/423358-tumarone-pervaya-polnocenno-rabotayushaya-bugbounty-ploshadka-v-sng); [digitalbusiness.kz Kcell](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-05-15/kcell-rasshiryaet-programmu-bug-bounty-uvelicheno-voznagrazhdenie-i-otkrit-dostup-ko-vsem-poddomenam/amp/); [digitalbusiness.kz Bereke](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-08-06/bereke-bank-zayavil-ob-usilenii-kiberbezopasnosti-s-pomoshchyu-etichnih-hakerov/amp/)
- **TrustLine (Riyadh)**: CB Insights says it was founded in 2021, so it is not a 2025 launch. — [CB Insights (via search)](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/bugbounter)
- **Cyber3ra (Nagpur, India)**: the Daily Swig covered its launch with an Oct 20 date (year not visible in the snippet; probably pre-2023, since the Daily Swig closed in 2023). It is not 2025. — [PortSwigger Daily Swig](https://portswigger.net/daily-swig/new-bug-bounty-platform-launches-for-indian-ethical-hackers)
- **Lupin & Holmes (Grenoble, France)** raised a $5.9M pre-seed in Mar 2026 (Seedcamp, 20VC, Kima, plus the founders of Hugging Face and Wiz). The founder is ex-bug-hunter Roni Carta. This is **not a crowd platform**: its product "Depi" maps supply-chain attack paths. Founded in 2023. — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/03/17/young-bug-bounty-hunter-bags-6m-pre-seed-round-for-security-startup/); [Startup Researcher](https://www.startupresearcher.com/news/bug-bounty-hunter-s-startup-lupin-and-holmes-secures-usd5-9-million-pre-seed)
- **Changes at existing platforms (not new platforms):** YesWeHack made its first acquisition (Sekost, Sep 2025) and launched "Agentic Pentest", an AI-agent PTaaS (Jun 2026). — [YesWeHack Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YesWeHack)

### Inferences
- In Web2, the 2025–26 market grew through **new programs on existing platforms and through AI features**, not through new crowd platforms that got funding. The few platforms that might be new are small, regional and anonymous, with no published payout data.
- Genuinely new entrants are most likely in places that need local currency payouts or local-language triage (Uzbekistan, Ukraine, Japan). That is a pattern, not a confirmed set of launches.

### Gaps
- No launch dates found for BBHunt, BugStream, OQDOPPI, UzHunter, ZeroDay Test, Hackr.fi (connection refused), Find The Gap, HuntersPay, BugRakshak or Cyberbay. WHOIS and Wayback checks were not run.
- The search budget ran out before I could check Product Hunt or Crunchbase for 2025 bug-bounty startups, or Reddit r/bugbounty threads.

## 2. New national / government bug bounty PLATFORMS 2025–2026

### Takeaway
I could not confirm any new **national platform** launched in 2025–2026. Governments launched new **programs** and **challenges** (Swiss e-ID, Pakistan NADRA, India UIDAI, US GSA TTS) and a new **EU procurement framework**. Ukraine's state use of BugStream and Uzbekistan's OQDOPPI are the closest leads to new national-level platforms, and both have unverified launch dates.

### Cited Findings
- **Switzerland, e-ID bug bounty**: launched July 2025 as a private program on the Swiss federal bug bounty platform and opened to the public in April 2026. The platform itself was procured in Aug 2022 after a 2021 pilot, so this is a new program, not a new platform. — [BACS/NCSC](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/en/bug-bounty-programme-to-increase-cyber-resilience-in-the-federal-administration); [NCSC page](https://www.ncsc.admin.ch/ncsc/en/home/infos-fuer/infos-it-spezialisten/themen/bug-bounty-programme.html)
- **Pakistan, NADRA Bug Bounty Challenge 2026**: NADRA's first such challenge, launched January 2026 with HEC and National CERT. It is a team competition open **only to universities and partner organisations** (27 teams, 88 participants; regional rounds from 27 Jan 2026). UET Lahore (Faisalabad) won. Third place (IIUI) received PKR 200,000. It is a one-off challenge, not an open platform. — [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/national/nadra-launches-bug-bounty-challenge-2026-to-strengthen-pakistans-digital-identity-security/); [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/02/17/uet-wins-first-ever-nadra-bug-bounty-challenge-2026/amp/); [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/nadra-launches-bug-bounty-challenge-to-fortify-pakistans-digital-id-infrastructure)
- **India, UIDAI (Aadhaar)**: launched its "first structured Bug Bounty Programme" in March 2026 with a selected panel of 20 researchers. Invite-only program. — [News On AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/uidai-launches-first-structured-bug-bounty-programme-to-strengthen-aadhaar-system-security)
- **USA, GSA Technology Transformation Service**: launched a bug bounty with HackerOne (reported Jul 2026). Program on an existing platform. — [ExecutiveGov](https://www.executivegov.com/articles/gsas-tech-transformation-service-launches-bug-bounty-program)
- **EU, European Commission (DG DIGIT) framework**: YesWeHack was ranked first in Oct 2025 under a 4-year framework worth up to €7,679,875. It covers bug bounties and VDPs for open-source projects and EU institutions. Programs under it include Jenkins (announced Dec 10, 2025) and OpenProject (ended by Jun 2026). Researchers earn through YesWeHack, which is not a new platform. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251001342504/en/YesWeHack-Won-the-European-Commissions-Latest-Bug-Bounty-Tender); [Jenkins blog](https://www.jenkins.io/blog/2025/12/10/jenkins-bug-bounty-program-yeswehack-european-commission/); [OpenProject](https://www.openproject.org/de/blog/bug-bounty-program-yes-we-hack/)
- **Saudi Arabia, CST**: published enrollment statistics for its BugBounty platform in Sep 2025. The platform dates to 2019 (SAFCSP), with corporate registration added at LEAP 2023. Not new. — [CST news 2025](https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/N2025091001); [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/bugbounty-platform)
- **UAE, National Bug Bounty Programme**: launched 1 Aug 2021 (NCSC, piloted with Etisalat/du). Wattle Corp says "January 2025", which conflicts with the primary 2021 coverage and is probably a misdating or an unverified relaunch. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/local/uae-cybersecurity-council-launches-national-bug-bounty-programme?web_view=true); [Wattle Corp](https://www.wattlecorp.com/?p=35134)
- **Maldives, NCSA**: runs a national bug bounty program. Its separate GRC platform went live in Oct 2025. I found no launch date for the bug bounty program itself. — [NCSA hub](https://hub.ncsa.gov.mv/)
- **Qatar, bugbounty.qa**: on the disclose list (private and public programs). I found no operator or launch announcement. Ooredoo's 2023 Qatar program ran on YesWeHack. — [disclose README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/disclose/bug-bounty-platforms/main/README.md); [Ooredoo PR](https://www.ooredoo.qa/web/en/press-release/ooredoo-launches-new-cyber-security-programme-to-upgrade-security-practices-and-enhance-cyber-security/)
- **Ukraine, BugStream / NCCC "DOT-Chain 2026" bug bash**: see section 1. — [DOU event](https://dou.ua/calendar/56405/)

### Inferences
- In 2025–26 governments mostly **reused existing platforms** (Swiss, EU via YesWeHack, US via HackerOne) or ran **closed or invite-only challenges** (Pakistan, India). None of these is an open earning venue for international researchers.
- bugbounty.qa and OQDOPPI may be recent national-level platforms, but I have no date evidence.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for the launch date or operator of bugbounty.qa, or for any government backing of OQDOPPI.
- I did not search other countries in their own languages (Vietnam, Indonesia, Morocco, Egypt, Nigeria, Philippines, Bangladesh). My English searches for Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Vietnam, Indonesia and Malaysia found only directory entries.

## 3. AI-era models: AI-hunter payouts, AI red-teaming arenas, hardware, OSS

### Takeaway
The real **2025 growth area for paid crowd work** is **AI red-teaming arenas and competitions**: Gray Swan Arena (ongoing, $350K+ distributed), HackAPrompt 2.0 (2025, OpenAI-sponsored), the Anthropic and OpenAI jailbreak bounties, and the Pwn2Own AI category (2025). No platform pays autonomous agents directly. AI hunters such as XBOW and other "hackbots" earn on HackerOne as ordinary accounts with human review.

### Cited Findings
- **Gray Swan Arena (app.grayswan.ai)** runs public AI red-teaming challenges paid in cash:
  - Agent Red-Teaming (8 Mar–6 Apr 2025): $171,800 prize pool, sponsored by UK AISI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind, about 1.8M attack attempts.
  - Machine-in-the-Middle (late 2025): $100k pool, with Hack The Box.
  - Visual Vulnerabilities: $60k, plus $100 for each of the first 50 first-time breakers.
  - "Proving Ground": a weekly series.
  - The site claims $350K+ distributed overall. Gray Swan's Reddit page renders $0, probably a stale or placeholder render.
  Public, open to everyone. The platform started in 2024, with its large 2025 events as the main draw. — [Gray Swan Arena](https://app.grayswan.ai); [Agent red-teaming](https://app.grayswan.ai/arena/challenge/agent-red-teaming); [Proving Ground](https://app.grayswan.ai/arena/blog/proving-ground-your-weekly-arena-for-ai-red-teaming-mastery); [casrai guide](https://casrai.org/guides/gray-swan-arena-crowdsourced-red-teaming)
- **HackAPrompt 2.0 (Learn Prompting x OpenAI, 2025)**: launched with "over $100,000 in prizes", including a CBRNE track ($50k, later $65k, extended to Jun 19) and an Agents track ($60k, from June). Decrypt claims $500k with $50k bounties, which conflicts with the organizer's figures. HackAPrompt joined the disclose list in Jul 2026. — [Learn Prompting](https://newsletter.learnprompting.org/p/new-hackaprompt-2-0-x-openai-largest-ai-red-teaming-competition-with-100-000-in-prizes-d426); [CBRNE update](https://newsletter.learnprompting.org/p/4-new-cbrne-challenges-a-65-000-prize-pool); contradicted by [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/323351/how-to-trick-chatgpt-and-get-paid-50000/)
- **Vendor AI jailbreak bounties (2025–26)**:
  - Anthropic: invite-only jailbreak bounty on HackerOne (May 2025), now concluded.
  - OpenAI GPT-5 Bio Bug Bounty (Sep 2025): up to $25k for a universal jailbreak.
  - OpenAI Safety Bug Bounty (Mar 25, 2026): public, on Bugcrowd.
  - Google AI VRP (Oct 2025): up to $30k.
  — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/testing-our-safety-defenses-with-a-new-bug-bounty-program); [OpenAI GPT-5 bio](https://openai.com/gpt-5-bio-bug-bounty/); [OpenAI Safety](https://openai.com/index/safety-bug-bounty/); [Infosecurity](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/google-launches-ai-bug-bounty/)
- **AI hunters on existing platforms**:
  - XBOW became #1 on HackerOne's US leaderboard (reputation) in Q2 2025 and #6 globally on Jun 25, 2025. It filed over 1,000 reports: 132 resolved, 303 triaged. Humans reviewed submissions to comply with HackerOne's AI policy. XBOW raised a $75M Series B (total $117M). — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-ai-xbow-tops-hackerone-us-leaderboad/); [XBOW blog](https://xbow.com/blog/series-b); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-06-24/one-of-the-best-hackers-in-the-country-is-an-ai-bot)
  - HackerOne's report (Jul 2024–Jun 2025): about $81M paid (+13%), 560 valid reports from autonomous "hackbots", valid AI-related reports up 210%, and 67% of researchers using AI tools. — [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/hackerone-bug-bounty-paid/)
  - Self-reported hackbot results: Joseph Thacker's team says its hackbot found 126 vulns in 5 months (Jul 2026 blog). — [josephthacker.com](https://josephthacker.com/hacking/2026/07/01/we-built-a-hackbot.html)
- **Pwn2Own Berlin 2025** added a first-ever **AI category**: $140k for AI-infrastructure exploits (Chroma, NVIDIA Triton, Container Toolkit), out of $1,078,750 total for 28 zero-days. It is an event by an existing broker (ZDI), not a new platform. — [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/hackers-earn-over-1-million-at-pwn2own-berlin-2025/); [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/178040/hacking/pwn2own-berlin-2025-total-prize-money-reached-1078750.html)
- **Microsoft Zero Day Quest**: the CSO list says it is "planned for spring 2026". I found no payout figures. — [CSO Online](https://www.csoonline.com/article/657751/top-bug-bounty-programs.html)
- **AI agent bounty payouts are small**: Anthropic ($100), GitHub ($500) and Google (undisclosed) quietly paid for prompt-injection hijacks of AI agents, with no CVEs. — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/ai-agents-hijacked-prompt-injection-bug-bounties-no-cve)
- **OSS/AI-library bounty platforms**: huntr (UK, AI/ML OSS) and 0din.ai (Mozilla, GenAI) are listed in the AI section of disclose. Both predate 2025. I found no new 2025–26 OSS platform. The EU-funded OSS bounties run on YesWeHack (section 2). — [disclose README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/disclose/bug-bounty-platforms/main/README.md)

### Inferences
- For an individual earner, the "new" venues of 2025–26 are **AI red-teaming prize arenas**, mainly Gray Swan (recurring, verified pools from major sponsors). Lumpier options are vendor AI bounties on HackerOne/Bugcrowd and HackAPrompt. Payouts are prize-based and concentrate among the top performers.
- AI-assisted hunting is allowed on HackerOne with human review, so "AI-hunter earnings" happen through existing platforms. I found no evidence of a dedicated pay-the-agent platform.

### Gaps
- No search budget was left for hardware, IoT, automotive or mobile-specific bounty platforms launched in 2025–26, or for DARPA AIxCC follow-on public competitions.
- Total payouts and current status of HackAPrompt 2.0 are unconfirmed.
- No Zero Day Quest 2025/2026 payout totals found.
